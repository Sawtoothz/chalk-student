Four figures, one idea: a motion model is a distribution over where a command takes the robot, and both of its forms (a sampler and a density) are only as good as their handling of the corners. Each figure has a Predict first prompt: write your guess down before you move the slider. Colours: blue = estimate / reference, coral = the book's version, teal = the quantity being taught, gray = structure, amber = warning. Every sampler is fed from one fixed list of 6000 standard normals, so the readouts equal those of session.py.
