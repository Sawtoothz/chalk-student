The iterated update with the information-form gain

C4 · C5

Same room, 2-D pose state (x, y, θ) with a prior P from propagation. Each iteration fits a line to the 5 nearest map points of every scan point, builds the m×3 Jacobian, and takes the step with K = (HᵀR⁻¹H + P⁻¹)⁻¹HᵀR⁻¹. The textbook gain is computed too, inverting the m×m matrix, so you can see they agree and what each one costs.
